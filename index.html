About the Artist

Eliz Stella

Artist & Founder, Eliz Studio

Eliz Stella is a self-taught resin artist whose work blends molten color, crushed crystal, and 24k gold leaf into one-of-a-kind statement pieces. Every pour at Eliz Studio is hand-poured, hand-finished, and designed to feel like a frozen moment of geode magic.

From luxury wall art to bespoke commissions, each creation carries the same philosophy: art should sparkle, surprise, and feel entirely your own.
